# Process Standards

## Intent flow
- Non-engineer intake runs the intent conversation → intent.md → tracker review (harbor four records) → accepted Intent hands to launch as its mission brief. Why: engineers only see fog-free, owner-signed goals.
- Conflicts surfaced during spec synthesis follow precedent-based routing: case-local answers stay in the spec; reusable business rules sediment into Rules pillar surfaces; hard-to-reverse technical tradeoffs become ADRs. Why: keeps one source of truth per lesson.
